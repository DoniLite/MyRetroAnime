# Textures

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain):

- `rock_face_03_*`: [Rock Face 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03)
- `aerial_grass_rock_*`: [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock)
- `snow_02_*`: [Snow 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02)

Color maps resized to 1024 px, normal maps (OpenGL convention) to 512 px.

## Trees (`trees/`)

Taken from [ez-tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree) (MIT License, Daniel Greenheck), resized:

- Bark: oak from [Poly Haven bark_brown_02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02) (CC0), birch and pine from [TextureCan](https://www.texturecan.com) (CC0).
- Leaves (`leaf_*.png`): ez-tree's leaf textures, converted to grayscale so the scene can tint them per season.
